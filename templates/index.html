Data Structures & Algorithms

  • Merge Sort & Quick Sort: Implemented manually in algorithms.py for candidate ranking.
  • Max-Heap (Priority Queue): Dynamic heap for $O(1)$ top candidate peek and $O(K \log N)$ top-K retrieval.
  • Inverted Index & Hash Maps: O(1) skill-to-candidate dictionary lookups.
  • Binary Search: Fast $O(\log N)$ candidate name retrieval on sorted sets.

Natural Language Processing

  • Tokenization & Normalization: Custom stop-word removal and punctuation cleaning.
  • Skill Taxonomy: Curated taxonomy covering 60+ technologies and canonical aliases.
  • TF-IDF & Cosine Metric: Pure Python vector dot products with L2 normalization.
  • Zero External Paid APIs: Fully self-contained and runnable offline.
